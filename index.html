    Recovered notes: the Ulerium prospectors

    Old prospectors converted Craponite into Ulerium, built improbable ships and dreamed of destroying ever larger celestial objects. Their notes mention Bacquelitte, Bullshit Crystals, Fotron torpedoes, pirates, space madness and the gods Caopectat and Gutalax.

    TOAST-9 has repurposed their synthesis process for a smaller ambition: a safe journey home. The reactor and containment systems keep that ambition manageable.

    These are the game's precursor notes, not extra objectives. Pirates and radiation are route lore in this campaign; there is no hidden combat or decay timer.
